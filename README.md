## What I am building toward

I work at the seam between **machine learning**, **decision science**, and **real-world deployment**: systems that do not just demo well, but survive handoffs, audits, messy data, latency constraints, and actual users.

My current focus is practical AI infrastructure for teams that need to reason over complex knowledge, evaluate model behavior, and ship reliable workflows: agentic RAG, graph-backed memory, ML workbenches, observability, automated evaluation, and design systems that make the output feel considered rather than generic.

## How I like to work

I like systems that are useful under pressure: observable, testable, documented, and easy to hand off. I care about the last mile as much as the model: the README, the evaluation harness, the interface, the story, and the operational path from prototype to trusted workflow.

The best projects here tend to combine three things: rigorous technical foundations, taste in the user-facing layer, and an honest account of what the system can and cannot do.
